01 THE WORLD

Between land and sea. A world of its own.

Quiet at first glance.
Alive with possibility.

Tiny tracks in the sand.
Fish darting through seagrass. Wildlife tucked away in tidal pools.

Tidal Atlas is a game about exploring tidal flats, observing wildlife, and collecting your discoveries. Bring the creatures you find into an aquarium and create a small world of your own.
